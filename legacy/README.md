# Legacy research notebooks (2025)

These notebooks are the original 2025 research project by **Pratosh Karthikeyan and Nirmal**:
CNN classification and U-Net segmentation of hemorrhage on brain CT renders.

They are kept for reference only:
- They are **not part of the current pipeline** (`src/brainct/`), are not linted or tested,
  and are not maintained.
- **Their reported numbers (e.g. 68% classification accuracy, 92% segmentation accuracy) are
  not comparable** to the current results: different data (JPG renders, not the original
  DICOMs), no patient-level split, and different metrics. Current, measured results are in
  [`docs/results.md`](../docs/results.md).
- They need data that is not in this repository.

Research use only, not for clinical decisions.
